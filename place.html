About Ghana

Ghana, located in West Africa, is known for its rich cultural heritage, vibrant traditions, and warm hospitality. From the bustling markets of Accra to the serene landscapes of the Volta Region, the country offers a blend of modernity and deep-rooted traditions.

Did you know? Ghana was the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence in 1957!
